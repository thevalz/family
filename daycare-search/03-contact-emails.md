## Sending tips
- **Email + phone both:** infant openings move fast; a quick call after emailing often gets you a real answer
  on waitlist position the same day.
- **Track every send** in `04-appointment-tracker.md` so nothing falls through.
- **Ask to be added to the waitlist on the first contact** — don't wait until after the tour. Many lists are
  free or low-deposit to join and you can decline later.
