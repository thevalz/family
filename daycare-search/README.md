# Birmingham Infant Daycare Search

A working package to identify, contact, visit, and compare infant daycares (ages 0–12 months)
in **Birmingham proper + Over the Mountain** (Homewood, Mountain Brook, Vestavia Hills, Hoover).

## Your search parameters
- **Care type:** Infant (0–12 months) — full-time
- **Geography:** City of Birmingham proper + Over the Mountain suburbs
- **Priorities (weighted highest in the matrix):** Curriculum/philosophy · Hours & flexibility · Cost · Accreditation & quality
- **Start date:** As soon as a spot opens (typical for infant waitlists)

## Files in this folder
| File | What it's for |
|------|----------------|
| `01-daycare-pool.md` | The full pool of candidates with addresses, phones, hours, and notes |
| `02-comparison-matrix.md` | Side-by-side comparison weighted on your four priorities |
| `03-contact-emails.md` | Ready-to-send inquiry emails (one master + per-center versions) |
| `04-appointment-tracker.md` | Track who you've emailed, replies, tours, and decisions |

## How to use this (suggested flow)
1. **Fill in your details once** — open `03-contact-emails.md` and complete the SENDER block at the top
   (phone, child's name/DOB). The drafts already use "Jon" and an ASAP start date.
2. **Send the emails** — start with the Tier-1 centers in the matrix. Infant spots in Birmingham
   are scarce and waitlisted, so getting on lists early matters more than picking the "perfect" one.
3. **Log replies in `04-appointment-tracker.md`** and book tours. Aim to visit 4–6 in person.
4. **Fill the blanks in `02-comparison-matrix.md`** during/after each tour (tuition, true availability,
   ratios, vibe). The matrix is built to be completed from real conversations — most centers won't
   publish infant tuition online.

## Important caveats (read before sending)
- **Tuition is almost never posted online** for infant care. Treat the cost column as "ask on the call."
  Regional benchmark: Birmingham metro infant care runs roughly **$720–$900/month** full-time.
- **UAB Early Learning Center** is **NAEYC-accredited with reduced tuition but open only to families with
  a parent employed at UAB / UAB Hospital.** You indicated no UAB employment, so it's listed for
  completeness but flagged ⛔ and no email is drafted for it.
- **IPC Day School** and some church preschools are **half-day / school-year-only** (not full-time daycare).
  Flagged 🕘 — useful as a backup or enrichment, not primary infant care.
- All availability, ratios, accreditation, and pricing should be **confirmed directly** — center details
  change and waitlists move fast.
