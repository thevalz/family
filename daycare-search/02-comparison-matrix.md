## Decision aids

**Weighting suggestion** (tune to taste): Curriculum ×2, Hours ×2, Cost ×2, Accreditation ×2,
everything else ×1. Multiply each /5 score by its weight, sum, and rank.

**Tie-breakers for infant care specifically:**
- **Ratio matters most for infants** — Alabama licensing allows up to 1:5 for infants; 1:3 or 1:4 is
  meaningfully better. Ask for the *actual* staffed ratio, not the licensed maximum.
- **Staff turnover** — consistent caregivers matter more for babies than facilities. Ask how long the
  infant-room lead has been there.
- **Waitlist reality** — the "best" center you can't get into for 14 months isn't an option. Get on
  2–3 waitlists now even while you tour.
