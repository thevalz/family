## To-confirm checklist (ask on first contact)
For every center: **(1)** current infant-room openings or waitlist length & position, **(2)** monthly
infant tuition + registration/deposit fees + sibling discounts, **(3)** exact open/close hours and
late-pickup policy, **(4)** infant teacher-to-child ratio and group size, **(5)** accreditation
(NAEYC / Alabama Quality STARS rating) and most recent state licensing inspection, **(6)** curriculum/
daily schedule for infants, **(7)** tour availability.
