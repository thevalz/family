## Questions to bring to every tour (quick checklist)
- [ ] Actual staffed infant ratio (not licensed max) and infant-room group size
- [ ] How long the infant-room lead teacher has been there / staff turnover
- [ ] Monthly tuition, registration, deposit, sibling discount — get it in writing
- [ ] Real waitlist position and realistic start date
- [ ] Sick policy, sleep/SIDS practices, drop-off security, daily-report app
- [ ] Accreditation (NAEYC) and Alabama Quality STARS rating + last licensing inspection
- [ ] Feeding/bottle/breastmilk handling and nap schedule flexibility for infants
